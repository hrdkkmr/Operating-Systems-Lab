Why consistent ordering eliminates circular wait?

Consistent resource ordering eliminates circular wait by forcing every process to acquire resources in the same predefined order.
For example, if Lock1 must always be acquired before Lock2, no process can hold Lock2 while waiting for Lock1.
Therefore, a circular chain of processes waiting for each other cannot be formed, which prevents deadlock.
